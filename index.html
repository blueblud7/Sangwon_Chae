// 01   About

Who I Am

I'm an engineering leader and researcher who has spent 15+ years at the intersection of deep systems engineering and strategic execution. My career spans the full stack of modern computing: mobile platforms at Samsung, GPU architecture at AMD, AI infrastructure at Moreh, and AI-first product development at IDS Display — where I now serve as Director of Engineering & IT.

At Samsung, I spent nearly a decade as Staff Software Engineer and Technical Leader, owning the full SDLC for flagship Galaxy apps — AR Emoji, Game Tuner, Game Launcher — deployed to over 1 billion devices globally. I managed teams of 30 engineers and filed over 45 patents in AI, mobile computing, and AR/VR.

My Ph.D. research at UC Irvine focused on HD-GraphViz: highly distributed graph visualization on large-scale tiled display walls — directly connecting my academic work to real systems challenges. During my PhD I also interned at Canon USA, developing multi-projector display systems and filing 5 patents in projection and display technology.

Today I build scalable AI/ML systems, lead teams through architectural and organizational shifts for AI-first development, and bridge the gap between engineering depth and executive strategy. I write about AI tools and building in public on Brunch (@kk2daddy) and LinkedIn.
